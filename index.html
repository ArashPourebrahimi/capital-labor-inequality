Model assumptions: 100 people belong to exactly one class; capitalists earn only capital income; employed workers share the entire labor-income allocation equally; unemployed workers receive no income or benefits; and capitalists within their group earn equally. Requested unemployment is rounded to a whole number of workers. At 100% unemployment, no worker can receive labor income: that share is recorded as unrealized, and actual distributed income falls below $100. The Gini is calculated from the actual income distribution, and is undefined if everyone has zero income. This simplified fixed-allocation model does not model unemployment's effect on production or changes in wages.
