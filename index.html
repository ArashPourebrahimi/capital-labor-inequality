Why this works

With two non-overlapping groups and equal income within each group, Gini = |α − s|, where α is capital's share of income and s is the capitalist share of the population. The Lorenz curve joins (0,0), the cumulative share of the lower-income group, and (1,1). Income per capita relative to the overall mean is α/s for capitalists and (1−α)/(1−s) for workers. These formulas assume nonnegative incomes. The share of capitalists is limited to 1%–99% so both groups exist.

This is a simplified teaching model, not an empirical estimate of inequality.
